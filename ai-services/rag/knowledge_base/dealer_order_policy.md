# Dealer and Distributor Commercial Policies

## Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)
- **Authorized Dealers:** Minimum order quantity is 50 assorted retail packs per order.
- **Regional Distributors:** Minimum order quantity is 500 packs or 50 kg total weight.
- **Bulk Discounts:** Orders exceeding 2,000 packs receive an additional volume rebate approved by management.

## Returns and Damaged Goods Policy
- **Damaged in Transit:** Breakage or puncture must be reported within 48 hours of shipment delivery with photographs.
- **Quarantine Requirement:** Returned goods are received into quarantine and are strictly unsellable until a formal warehouse and quality review is completed (Invariant B7).
- **Payment & Invoicing:** Standard credit terms for verified dealers are 15 days from invoice issuance.
