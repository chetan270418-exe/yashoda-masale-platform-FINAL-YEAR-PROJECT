# Spice Storage and Shelf Life Guidelines

## General Storage Recommendations
- **Temperature:** Store in cool, ambient temperatures between 15°C and 25°C. Keep away from cooking ranges, stoves, or direct heat sources.
- **Humidity & Moisture:** Keep relative humidity below 65%. Never insert a wet spoon into powdered spice containers.
- **Sunlight Protection:** Direct UV sunlight breaks down curcumin in turmeric and bleaches the carotenoid pigments in red chilli powder. Always store in opaque or dark containers.

## Warehouse & Retailer Best Practices
- Keep cartons elevated on wooden or plastic pallets at least 15 cm above floor level.
- Maintain First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) stock rotation.
- Expired or damaged goods must be isolated in quarantine zones and must never be sold or dispatched.
