# Yashoda Masale Product Specifications

## Yashoda Pure Turmeric Powder (Haldi)
- **Origin & Ingredients:** 100% pure dried turmeric rhizomes sourced from Salem and Sangli regions.
- **Active Curcumin:** Guaranteed minimum of 3.0% natural curcumin content.
- **Color & Aroma:** Deep golden yellow, warm earthy aroma with subtle peppery notes.
- **Quality Parameters:** Moisture content strictly under 9.5%. Certified free of artificial coloring agents, lead chromate, and metanil yellow.
- **Available Pack Sizes:** 100g, 250g, and 500g food-grade multi-layer foil pouches.
- **Shelf Life:** 12 months from manufacturing date when stored in airtight packaging.

## Yashoda Special Garam Masala
- **Composition:** Authentic 16-spice formulation including green cardamom, black cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, mace, nutmeg, black pepper, cumin seeds, coriander seeds, and bay leaves.
- **Processing:** Slow-roasted at controlled temperatures (<60°C) prior to slow stone-grinding to prevent volatile essential oil dissipation.
- **Aroma & Flavor:** Complex warm, sweet-spicy bouquet; intensely fragrant.
- **Available Pack Sizes:** 50g, 100g, 250g, and 500g pouches.
- **Shelf Life:** 9 months from manufacturing date.

## Yashoda Red Chilli Powder (Mirchi Powder)
- **Blend:** Balanced formulation of Guntur chillies (for pungent heat) and Byadgi chillies (for rich natural crimson red color).
- **Color Value:** Minimum 100 ASTA color units.
- **Heat Level:** Moderate-high (approx. 25,000–30,000 Scoville Heat Units).
- **Available Pack Sizes:** 100g, 250g, 500g, and 1kg bulk bags.
- **Shelf Life:** 12 months from manufacturing date.
